# Penance

**Part II of the Boogeyman series**
by [gon pulvo](https://gonpulvo.com)

*Penance* is the companion story to *Boogeyman*. Where *Boogeyman* follows a dreamer losing control of a world shaped by fear, *Penance* deals with the cost of trying to fix it.

What happens when someone who can alter a world begins deciding what that world should be? And what is owed to the beings who already live there?

---

## About the Project

*Penance* is an experimental electronic album and narrative project built through M8 Community Jam prompts, dream logic, dialogue, environmental storytelling, and sound design.

The story is intentionally nonlinear. Events may appear as memories, projections, possibilities, reconstructed experiences, or direct events.

At the center of it all is a simple problem: **every solution costs something.**

---

# Characters

## Creator

The dreamer and original creator of the world. He wants to wake up, but escaping becomes increasingly complicated once the world and the people inside it stop behaving like disposable parts of a dream.

## Squid

A created being originally designed to help Creator survive. Squid embodies an intensified version of Creator's ideals: autonomy, determination, freedom, survival, and self-expression without many of Creator's limitations.

## Agnes

A recurring witness to the history of the dream world. Agnes understands people, events, and consequences that Creator often encounters only in fragments.

## Oisin

A sorcerer whose understanding of magic exceeds Creator's. Through Oisin, magic becomes less of a mysterious ability and more of a system with rules, history, and consequences.

## Pastor

A figure caught within the increasingly unstable relationship between identity, power, and survival. His existence becomes part of the larger question of what happens when beings inside this world are changed by forces they cannot fully control.

## Cornflake

Squid's longtime companion and frequent witness to his decisions. Cornflake provides another perspective on a world becoming increasingly strange around them.

---

# Magic

Magic in *Penance* is not free. Changing reality requires something from reality, and larger interventions carry larger consequences.

A recurring concern is **potentiality**: what possibilities disappear when one possibility is forced into existence?

# The Central Problem

Creator wants to wake up. Easy enough, except the dream now contains beings with memories, relationships, ambitions, fears, and their own reasons for wanting to exist.

If Creator leaves, what happens to them? If he stays, what happens to him? If he changes the system so everybody gets what they want, what does that change cost?

That's *Penance*.

---

# Power and Responsibility

Nearly every major system in *Penance* asks the same question in a different way: **who carries the burden?**

The robot sustains a civilization. Oisin manipulates life and death. Creator alters magic. Squid pursues autonomy. Agnes remembers the consequences.

Nobody gets to stand completely outside the system.

---

# Rain

Rain represents the movement and redistribution of power. Instead of remaining concentrated in one person or place, magic begins circulating through the environment.

Redistribution does not remove power. It changes who can access it.

---

# Limits

*Penance* repeatedly treats limits as necessary rather than inherently restrictive. Unlimited power allows one person, idea, or system to overwrite everything around it.

The question becomes less **"How powerful can I become?"** and more **"How much power can I hold without preventing everyone else from existing?"**

# Relationship to Boogeyman

*Boogeyman* asks what happens when parts of the self begin behaving like independent beings.

*Penance* asks what responsibility follows once they do.
